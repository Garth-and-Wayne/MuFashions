Sandwich
Written by Tyler Wolfe — Key of G — ~160 BPM

Written in a single sitting, arranged by the full band within a week of being heard. The chord progression is deceptively simple — four chords that have been used a thousand times, in service of a lyric that has never existed before and will never exist again. Recorded live in the room. One take on the vocals. You can hear someone laugh at the end of the second chorus. It was left in.

You Already Ate (2000)
Open The Door
(It's Not Locked)
Written by Scott Heuke — Key of E — ~140 BPM
Chords Used
E A B C#m

The only song on the debut written entirely by Scott Heuke, reportedly in response to a locked door that, as it turned out, was not locked. The metaphor is either very deep or completely absent. Heuke has declined to clarify. The opening riff is the catchiest thing on the record and everyone knows it, including Heuke, which is why he never mentions it.
